The hybrid search in src/search/hybrid.rs over-fetches KNN results by 3x (knn_limit = limit * 3) to compensate for post-KNN status and type filtering. This is necessary because sqlite-vec does not support JOINs with regular tables, so filtering happens in Rust after the KNN search returns. The batch-fetch pattern (get_entities_batch) avoids N individual get_entity calls.
