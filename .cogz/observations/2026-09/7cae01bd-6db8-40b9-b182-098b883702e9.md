OnnxEmbeddingModel in src/embed/onnx.rs uses lazy loading — the ONNX session and tokenizer are Mutex<Option<...>> fields that start as None and are populated on first embed() or embed_query() call. The has_token_type_ids field defaults to true and is dynamically detected from session.inputs() during try_load(). CodeRankEmbed does not declare token_type_ids; BERT-based models do.
