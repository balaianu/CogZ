The is_available() method on OnnxEmbeddingModel returns false before the first successful load. This caused a bug in hooks/lifecycle.rs where assemble_pack gated query embedding on is_available(), resulting in FTS-only context packs from hooks. Fixed by removing the gate and calling embed_query() directly — the error is handled by .ok().
