The initial `fit_budget` implementation truncated content to
the remaining token budget without accounting for the title's
token cost. This meant a section with a long title and truncated
content could exceed the budget by `title_tokens` tokens.

**Example:** Budget = 100, used = 99, remaining = 1. Title =
"Rule with a very long title" (28 chars = 7 tokens). Content
truncated to 1 token (4 chars). Total section tokens = 8, but
budget only had 1 token remaining. Pack exceeded budget by 7
tokens.

**Fix:** Subtract `estimate_tokens(&section.title)` from the
remaining budget before calculating the content truncation
budget:

```rust
let title_cost = estimate_tokens(&section.title);
let content_budget = remaining.saturating_sub(title_cost);
```

When `content_budget` is 0, the content becomes empty (no
ellipsis). The section is still kept with just the title — this
is intentional, as the title alone may be useful context.
